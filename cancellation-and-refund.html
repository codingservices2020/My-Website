Cancellation & Refund Policy

Transparent policies for cancellations, refunds, and revisions.

📅 Last Updated: May 11, 2025  |  Effective Date: May 11, 2025

1. Overview

At CodingServices.co.in, we are committed to delivering high-quality digital academic and document support services. Because our services involve immediate digital delivery, our cancellation and refund policy is structured around the stage at which work has been completed.

2. Order Cancellation

You may request a cancellation under the following conditions:

  • Within 2 hours of placement: Full refund if work has not yet been initiated.
  • After work has started: Partial refund may be issued based on the percentage of work completed.
  • After delivery: No cancellation is applicable once the final deliverable has been sent.

All cancellation requests must be submitted in writing via our Contact page or by email.

3. Refund Eligibility

✔ Eligible for Refund

  • Order cancelled before work begins
  • Duplicate payment made
  • Service not delivered within agreed timeframe
  • Deliverable significantly different from agreed scope

✘ Not Eligible for Refund

  • Work already delivered as agreed
  • Dissatisfaction due to change of mind
  • Documents submitted with incorrect details
  • Delays caused by client's late submission

4. Refund Process

1
Submit a Request

Contact us via the Contact page with your order ID and reason for refund.

2
Review (2–3 Business Days)

Our team reviews your request and evaluates eligibility based on the service stage and circumstances.

3
Decision & Communication

We will inform you of the outcome via email. Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method.

4
Refund Credit (5–10 Business Days)

Approved refunds are typically credited within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or payment provider.

5. Revisions Policy

We offer free revisions to ensure your satisfaction. If the delivered service does not match the scope agreed upon at the time of order, you may request a revision within 7 days of delivery. Revision requests are reviewed within 1–2 business days.

Revisions are limited to corrections within the original scope. Requests that expand the scope may be treated as new orders.

6. International Refunds

For international clients, refunds are processed in the original transaction currency. Please note that exchange rate fluctuations between the time of payment and refund are beyond our control. Any transaction fees charged by your bank or payment provider are non-refundable by us.

7. Contact for Refund Queries

For any questions related to cancellations or refunds, please contact our support team. We aim to resolve all queries fairly and promptly.
