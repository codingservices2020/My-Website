Shipping & Exchange Policy

How we deliver our digital services to you, anywhere in the world.

📅 Last Updated: May 11, 2025  |  Effective Date: May 11, 2025
🌐

100% Digital Delivery

CodingServices.co.in provides exclusively digital services. There is no physical shipping involved. All deliverables — reports, edited documents, analyses — are delivered electronically to your registered email address or via a secure download link.

1. Nature of Our Services

All services offered by CodingServices.co.in are digital in nature. This includes:

  • Plagiarism analysis reports (PDF/digital format)
  • AI writing detection reports
  • Proofread and edited documents
  • Formatted documents (Word, PDF)
  • Citation-corrected manuscripts
  • Language-edited academic papers

No physical goods are manufactured, packaged, or shipped under any circumstances.

2. Delivery Method

Completed deliverables are sent:

  • Via Email: Delivered to the email address provided at the time of order
  • Secure Download Link: A time-limited secure link shared via email

Clients are responsible for ensuring their email address is correct and that our emails are not filtered as spam.

4. Delays

Delivery timelines may be affected by:

  • Late submission of required documents by the client
  • Incomplete or unclear order instructions
  • High-volume periods (we will notify you in advance)

If a delay is caused on our end, we will notify you promptly and, where applicable, offer a partial refund or expedited completion at no extra charge. See our Cancellation & Refund Policy for details.

5. Exchange / Re-delivery Policy

As our services are digital, "exchange" refers to revision or re-delivery of the deliverable. You are entitled to request re-delivery or revision if:

  • The file received is corrupted or incomplete
  • The wrong document was delivered
  • The deliverable does not reflect the agreed-upon scope of work

All re-delivery or revision requests must be submitted within 7 days of the original delivery date.

6. International Clients

We serve clients globally. Since all deliveries are digital, there are no customs fees, import duties, or international shipping charges. However, clients are responsible for ensuring that receiving our services complies with any local regulations in their country.

7. Contact for Delivery Issues

If you experience any issues with delivery, please contact our support team immediately. We aim to resolve delivery-related issues within 1 business day.
